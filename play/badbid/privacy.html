BAD BID.
Privacy

Last updated September 30, 2026

BAD BID collects nothing about you. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved run, your settings, and your Family Album (stats, achievements, the characters you’ve met, your run log).
  • They live only in your browser’s local storage on this device. We never see them, and they are not sent anywhere.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes them.

What the game downloads

Only its own files: the game itself, its pictures, voices, sounds and music. Nothing is loaded from anyone else’s servers.

Sharing your run log

The Album’s “Send the run log” button hands a plain-text summary of your runs to your phone’s share menu or clipboard. Where it goes from there is up to you; the game doesn’t send it anywhere on its own.

Children

The game doesn’t ask for or collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example, online play — this page will say so before it happens.
