About Deep Dig

Made for Tori and TJ. The layers are drawn at their true sizes (the crust really is that thin). Every fact shown comes from NASA, USGS, National Geographic Education, Britannica or Oregon State's Volcano World — see SOURCES.md in the project.

  • Earth map and clouds: Solar System Scope textures (CC BY 4.0, solarsystemscope.com/textures), based on NASA imagery. Colours adjusted.
  • The digger, volcano and everything inside the Earth are drawn in code. The digger is pretend — no machine could dig down there.
  • Honest simplifications: the volcano is drawn 2× taller than it is wide above ground; its magma story is sped up from years to seconds; the mantle's slow creep is sped up too.
